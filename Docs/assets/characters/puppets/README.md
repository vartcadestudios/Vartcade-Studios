## v2 fixes (from Andre's notes)
- Eyes: the sketches left the eye openings see-through; they now have eye whites, an iris, a pupil and a catch light.
- Long hair (C1, C4) is on its own `hairback` layer behind the shoulders, so it can sway. C1's flat
  cut at the bottom is replaced by tapered ends that fall behind the back.
- Default outfit (v2.2): a dark grey long-sleeved top with a crew neck (the tank top looked odd); the neck shows
  the character's own skin. A brass paper-fastener marks the shoulder joint, like a paper puppet.
  The seat number is a faint cream stencil on the chest (the teal jumpsuit and zip are gone).
- Seats on the left of the table are mirrored so they look in toward the middle (`Puppet.FaceRight`);
  the seat number on the chest stays readable.

## The rig (Puppet.cs)
Torso breathes; head nods from the neck and tilts toward the pointer; eyelids blink and stay shut at
Night; the front arm lifts now and then; long hair follows the head and swings a little behind it.
