# Style notes from the reference pack (Nov 26)

What the team collected, and what we take from each folder. Blank folders were filled from the
overall direction. The reference images themselves are NOT in the repo: they belong to other
games and artists and are for inspiration only.

## 01 Overall style
- **60 Seconds!**: sickly yellow-green daylight, scrappy fences, crooked poles, a 1950s satire tone.
- **Sally Face**: muted, dirty colours; cluttered rooms full of posters and junk; thick uneven lines; eerie faces.
- **Fran Bow**: dark vignette at the screen edges; one warm light in a cold room; slightly wrong proportions.
- **Fallout Shelter**: cheerful characters in a bleak world; numbered vault suits; poster mascots.
- **Take:** dirty warm light, heavy vignette, clutter at the edges, cheerful mascot against grim setting.

## 02 UI and HUD
- Chunky cartoon UI: thick dark outlines, bevelled buttons with a light top edge and a dark bottom
  lip, wood or metal panels with screws, big rounded plates for titles.
- Lettering: heavy condensed display type with a 3D extrude and a darker outline (like the card
  titles and the 60 Seconds logo). Body text stays plain and readable.
- **Take:** match the UI buttons and titles to the card frames: gold/orange plates, red extrude,
  dark outline. Keep the palette; add bevel, outline and drop shadow.

## 03 Cards
Our own art (see `../cards`). Fronts carry a sun corner icon, backs a moon: keep that rule.

## 04 Characters
- Post-apocalyptic layered clothes: jackets over hoodies, patched trousers, scarves, goggles, gas masks.
- Rex's accessory sketches: glasses (round, square, sunglasses), necklaces, caps, beanies, headbands,
  ear headbands, top hat. These feed the store list (S01).
- Kitty Powers' Matchmaker: customisation as a grid of priced items, big Back / Continue buttons.

## 05 Environments
- Bunker: curved concrete walls, metal shelves of cans and jugs, a single hanging bulb, a cluttered
  table, posters and warnings on the wall.
- Outside: empty road to the horizon, broken billboards, leaning power poles, dead trees, hazy sun.
- Lobby references: framed card slots and a SPIN wheel; bold frames for the seat cards.
- Night: deep blue-green, moonlight, eerie trees (Fran Bow).

## 06 Filipino props (blank: filled from the brief)
Sardine and corned beef cans, rice sack, blue water galon, tabo and balde, banig, electric fan,
transistor radio, Chinese-drugstore wall calendar, tingi sachet strips, monobloc chairs,
hand-painted BAWAL signs, jeepney, sari-sari store shutter. All brands made up.

## 07 Brand: Silong Shelters
- Rubber hose motion: bendy limbs, white gloves, pie-cut eyes, bouncy walk.
- Poster layout like retro vault posters: big slogan on top, mascot centre, slogan at the bottom,
  two-colour print with worn paper texture.
- **Rule:** the sun mascot is our own design; no Cuphead or Vault Boy features.

## 08 Animation and motion
- Puppets: cutout parts with simple pivots (Crazy Dave, Scribblenauts, Kitty Powers idle): talk by
  bobbing the head, blink, small arm gestures, a speech bubble.
- Cards: UNO-style fanned hand and big centre play; smooth slides between hands.
- Transitions: a tearing calendar page for each new Day (now in the game as PhaseCalendar);
  day-to-night as a sky wipe.
- Rondo: a big wheel with icons per slice; each disaster gets a short looping badge animation
  (blood moon, dehydration, spoiled food, radioactive storm, toxic fog).

## 09 Lighting
- One flickering bulb as the main light; warm pool, dark corners; night = cool blue with a vignette.

## 10 Sound and mood (links in the reference pack)
Five tracks: epic post-apocalyptic, sad pre-war with a beat, poetic and thematic, energetic punk,
rising pressure. Use as mood targets for P01 (calm Days 1-4, uneasy 5-7, tense 8-10, Night).
