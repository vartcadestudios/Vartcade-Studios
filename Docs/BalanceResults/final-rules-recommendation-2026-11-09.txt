TEN DAYS TO SUNDOWN - WHAT TO COMMIT IN UNITY
=============================================
Recommendation based on every Balance Simulator test so far (tens of thousands of games),
using forgetful (casual) players. Bots are not people, so treat this as the starting point
for playtests, not the final word.


1. COMMIT THESE (keep or add)
-----------------------------
Already in the Unity build, keep them:
  a) Survive with the Key OR 3 DIFFERENT Supply kinds.
     Makes collecting the real goal; ties and "full bunker" losses dropped from about 27% to 10%.
  b) 20 + 20 deck: 4 of each Morning card; 8 Flashlight, 6 Night Skip, 6 Day Skip backs.
  c) One-use Flashlight that shows a whole hand (yours too).
     Played about 1.4-1.9 times a game, about as strong as the old 3-battery version, easier to read.
  d) Played Morning cards stay in your hand (each front works once).
     Morning cards went from losing cards (about 26% win rate when played) to useful ones (about 44%).
  e) Catch-up draw from Day 2 for whoever is furthest behind.
     Hopeless last Days dropped from about 23% to 5-8%. This matters even more with the card spread below.
  f) Key in the top half of the draw pile.
     The Key shows up in about 96% of games instead of about 50%; "nobody survives" almost disappears.
  g) Doomsday Clock can't land on the last Day (it did nothing there).
  h) 2 bunker seats, 3 with 5-6 players.
     At 6 players each player's chance goes from about 30% to about 45%.

Add next:
  i) A FIXED CARD SPREAD instead of random front/back pairing.
     Use your spread, but with 7 real / 4 Trash per Supply kind (instead of 6 / 5):
       Each Supply kind (13 cards): 7 real Supply, 4 Trash, 1 Night Skip, 1 Day Skip back
       = 28 real / 16 Trash / 4 Night Skip / 4 Day Skip on Supply fronts
       Morning fronts (20): 2 Supply, 6 Trash, 8 Flashlight, 2 Night Skip, 2 Day Skip backs
     Why 7/4 and not 6/5:
       6/5: very balanced, but about 17-20% of players are hopeless on the last Day.
       7/4: hopeless drops to about 11-13%, ties stay low (about 17%), 1.77 survivors per game.
       8/3 or more: 3 kinds gets too easy and ties start deciding games again.
     If the team wants a harsher, tenser game, 6/5 is the fallback; nothing else needs changing.


2. NIGHT RULES: KEEP THE CURRENT ONES, BUT EXPLAIN THEM BETTER
--------------------------------------------------------------
Tested: current (2 Night actions, a peek costs 1, must peek once)
   vs new (1 Night action, all peeks free).
- With the random deck, the new rules were more balanced (skill gap 15 vs 21).
- With the card spread you'd actually use, they come out about even (gap 15 vs 14),
  and the new rules cost more:
     more real Supply lost per game (2.3 vs 1.3, from more Swaps),
     more hopeless players (20% vs 17%),
     about 50% longer waiting (12 vs 8 minutes per game).
So keep the current Night rules. They test exactly the same as
"1 free peek, then 1 action", which is easier to teach. Write the rule as:
   NIGHT: Peek at one of your cards (free, required), then take 1 action:
          Steal, Swap, peek again, or play a Night card.
          You may Steal or Swap only once a Night.
Nothing changes in the code for this; it's the same rule worded more simply
(update the How to Play text and the FC docs).


3. DO NOT COMMIT THESE (tested and they don't help)
---------------------------------------------------
- Resource conversion: helps strong players more, and players accidentally convert the Key.
- Blind steal, neighbours-only steal, steal protection: almost no change.
- First Night card free: no change.
- 1 Night action with paid peeks: deletes Steal, Swap and Night cards entirely.
- No drawing (game breaks: nobody survives 80% of the time) and a free card every Day
  (hands flood and ties decide everything).
- The strong Rondo on its own: too harsh (nobody survives in about 14% of games).


4. STILL OPEN (decide later, after people playtest)
---------------------------------------------------
- Stealing is the biggest frustration in every test (about 4 cards per player per game).
  Next ideas worth testing: the victim learns who stole, or Steal costs the thief a card.
- Night and Day Skip cards are rarely played (under 1 per game) because they're hidden on backs.
  Idea: put some on Morning fronts.
- Waiting: about 8 minutes per game at 4 players and 14 at 6. Keep peeks and animations quick
  (a turn timer would help).
- Play real games with classmates (requests Q07 and Q15) to check how stealing actually feels.


5. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE UNITY BUILD
--------------------------------------
- Items a-h are in the code (on the local branch feature/balance-results).
- Item i is now in the game too: the deck is built from the fixed card spread
  (7 real / 4 Trash per kind) instead of random front/back pairing. The Card Gallery
  and the Balance Simulator use the same deck.
- Item 2: the How to Play Night text now reads "two actions, and one must be a Peek".
  The FC01-FC03 docs still need the same update.
- The FC docs are out of date on a, b, d-i and need new section 7 rows.
- GitHub: nothing new is pushed yet. When the rules are agreed, push the branch and open a
  pull request into develop (main only changes through a reviewed pull request).
