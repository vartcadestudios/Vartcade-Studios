TEN DAYS TO SUNDOWN - CARD SPREAD TEST
======================================
Balance Simulator, 1,000 games per setup, 4 players unless noted.
All tests: 3 different Supply kinds to qualify, kept Morning cards, catch-up draw,
Key in the top half of the pile, 3 bunker seats at 5-6 players, forgetful (casual) players.

Current rules = 2 Night actions, each peek costs 1 action (the game now)
New rules     = 1 Night action, all peeks free

YOUR CARD SPREAD (checked card by card)
Supply-front cards, for EACH kind (Food, Water, Medicine, Entertainment), 13 per kind:
   6 real Supply back, 5 Trash back, 1 Night Skip back, 1 Day Skip back
   Total on Supply fronts: 24 real / 20 Trash / 4 Night Skip / 4 Day Skip
Morning-front cards (20, 4 of each), backs spread evenly over the five Morning cards:
   2 Supply, 6 Trash, 8 Flashlight, 2 Night Skip, 2 Day Skip
Whole deck: 8 Flashlight, 6 Night Skip, 6 Day Skip, plus the Bunker Key.
Note: the 2 Supply backs on Morning cards are left out of the 24/20 count, as asked,
but in play they count as real Supply (one Food, one Water) because their back is Supply.

The "Random pairing" rows are the game's current deck for comparison:
36 real Supply and 16 Trash backs, with fronts and backs paired at random.

WHAT YOUR SPREAD CHANGES (compared with the current random deck)
---------------------------------------------------------------
- Harder to survive. Fewer real Supply cards (24 + 2 instead of 36), so:
  survivors per game drop from about 1.85 to about 1.7, and about 46-49% of players
  now lose because they can't collect 3 kinds (was about 24-26%).
- Much fewer tie losses: only about 10-12% lose a tie or find the bunker full (was about 28-29%).
  Reaching 3 kinds is the real challenge again.
- More balanced under the current rules: Aggressive drops from 57% to 48%, the skill gap
  shrinks from 21 to 14 points. Under the new rules the gap stays about 15.
- The Key matters more: about 57% of survivors get in by the Key (was about 51-52%).
- Less Supply lost to stealing: 1.3 per player per game under the current rules (was 3.0),
  because there's less real Supply around to steal.
- Main downside: many more players are hopeless going into the last Day:
  about 17% (current rules) and 20% (new rules), up from about 4-5%.

NEW RULES vs CURRENT RULES, WITH YOUR SPREAD
-------------------------------------------
- Similar survivors (1.67 vs 1.70) and a similar skill gap (15 vs 14).
- New rules lift Human-like and Passive (47% and 44%) and lower Aggressive (43%).
  Current rules: Aggressive 48%, Human-like 46%, Passive 42%.
- New rules: fewer steals (3.7 vs 4.4 cards) but more Supply lost overall (2.3 vs 1.3),
  because free peeking makes Swaps more targeted.
- New rules: more Flashlights and Skip cards played (3.2 and 0.6 per game vs 1.4 and 0.2).
- New rules: a bit more hopelessness (20% vs 17%) and longer waiting (about 12 vs 8 min).

DIFFERENT REAL / TRASH SPREADS (Tables 5 and 6)
-----------------------------------------------
- 3-4 real per kind (12-16 real): too harsh. Only 1.2-1.4 survivors, 36-51% hopeless,
  and luck decides more than skill (gap only 7-9 points).
- 5 real per kind (20 real): still harsh, 25-30% hopeless.
- 6 real per kind (your spread, 24 real): 17-20% hopeless, ties rare.
- 7 real per kind (28 real): the best middle ground. 1.77 survivors, 11-13% hopeless,
  ties 16-17%, gap 16-18 points, and about 39-40% still lose for lacking 3 kinds.
- 8-9 real per kind (32-36 real): easy to reach 3 kinds; ties decide more (24-32%)
  and hopelessness is low (3-7%).

WHAT STAYED THE SAME AS PREVIOUS TESTS
--------------------------------------
- Stealing is still about 3.7-4.9 cards per player per game, the biggest frustration.
- Rondo losses about 1.1-1.3, Skip turns about 0.3-0.4, refused trades about 3 per player.
- Turn order is close to fair (first 43%, last 44% with your spread).
- The new rules are still at least as balanced as the current rules, and slower.
- Night and Day Skip cards are still rarely played (well under 1 per game), even with more
  of them on Supply fronts: they're hidden on backs and players spend Night actions elsewhere.

SUGGESTION
----------
Your spread works, but it leaves about 1 in 5 players with no hope on the last Day.
Moving to 7 real / 4 Trash per kind (28 real / 16 Trash) keeps most of the benefits
(rare ties, balanced strategies) while cutting hopeless players to about 1 in 8.
