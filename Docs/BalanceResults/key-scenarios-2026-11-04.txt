TEN DAYS TO SUNDOWN - KEY TEST RESULTS
======================================
Balance Simulator, 1,000 games per setup, 4 players unless noted.
All tests: 20 + 20 deck, 3 different Supply kinds to qualify, kept Morning cards,
catch-up draw, 3 bunker seats at 5-6 players, forgetful (casual) players.

New rules      = 1 Night action, all peeks free
Previous rules = 2 Night actions, each peek costs 1 action

Key cases:
  Normal        = Key shuffled into the top half of the draw pile (the game)
  Key drawn     = one player is dealt the Key at the start
  Key discarded = the Key is face up in the discard pile from the start
  Key not drawn = the Key is at the bottom of the draw pile

(Tables line up best in a monospace font, e.g. Notepad.)


WHAT NORMALLY HAPPENS TO THE KEY
--------------------------------
In a player's hand at Sundown ...... 96% of games
Removed by Purge ...................  4% of games
Never drawn ........................ about 1 game in 1,000

WHAT CHANGES WITH THE KEY
-------------------------
Key drawn (dealt at start):
- Almost the same as normal games, because the Key reaches a hand 96% of the time anyway.
- Turn order is fairer (nobody gets the Key just by drawing more).
- The Key changes hands more often (0.78 losses per player per game instead of 0.52).

Key discarded or not drawn (no Key):
- Fewer survivors, and nobody survives in about 3% of games instead of almost 0%.
- More players fall short of 3 Supply kinds (31% instead of 26%).
- More players are hopeless on the last Day (5.8% instead of 4.5%).
- Skill matters more: the gap between best and worst strategy grows by about 3 points.
- New rules: Human-like loses most. Previous rules: Passive loses most.
- Discarded and not drawn give the same result at 4 players: the pile never runs out,
  so a Key at the bottom never comes up. At 6 players it sometimes does.
- The bots play the same whether or not they know the Key is gone. Real players who
  know it was discarded would stop chasing it, so "discarded" may play a bit differently.


NEW RULES vs PREVIOUS RULES
---------------------------
Different:
- New rules are more balanced in every Key case (skill gap 15-19 instead of 18-25).
  Passive does better, Aggressive does worse.
- More cards lost: about 1 more real Supply per game to Swaps (4.1 vs 3.0), and about
  4 more peeked cards taken away (10 vs 6.4), because people peek and Swap more.
- More refused trades (3.3 vs 2.8) and more Skip turns (0.46 vs 0.36).
- Longer waiting: about 12 min per game instead of 8 (fast peeking in the game would help).

The same:
- Stealing is still about 4-5 cards per player per game, the biggest frustration.
- Rondo losses (about 1.3), scrambled hands (about 0.5), turns with nothing to do (about 0.2).
- Endings: about 30% collapse at the end, 26-30% lose with 3 kinds, about 5% hopeless.
- These match the earlier free-peek test (within about 3 points).


BOTTOM LINE
-----------
- 1 Night action with free peeks is the more balanced rule set, whatever happens to the Key.
- It costs more lost cards (Swaps) and a longer game.
- Without the Key, fewer people survive and skill matters more.
- Stealing is still the biggest frustration under both rule sets.
